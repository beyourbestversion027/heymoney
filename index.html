Sync Across Devices (Manual)
HeyMoney doesn't sync automatically — your phone and laptop each keep their own data. To move data from one to the other: tap Share Backup on the device with your latest data, send it to yourself (WhatsApp, email, Drive — whatever's easiest), then open that file on the other device and use Import. Importing replaces that device's data entirely, so always export/share from whichever device has your newest entries.
Backup & Restore
JSON keeps everything (incl. Split trips, Money Owed and Net Worth entries) with full fidelity. CSV is available for a simple spreadsheet-friendly export. Everything stays local.
Auto-Backup
Automatically downloads a JSON backup to your device when you open the app, if it's overdue. This can't run in the background on a schedule — it only checks when you actually open HeyMoney. For real protection, occasionally move these downloaded files off this device (Drive, email to yourself, etc.) — clearing your browser/app data wipes everything stored here.
Erase all local data
Deletes all trips, PIN settings, and logo stored in this browser. Cannot be undone.
